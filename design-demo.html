Four Depths · three design directions

What is there now. A cream page, a serif title and a terracotta accent. It is clean, but it is the house style of every quiet blog, and it says nothing about the one idea that makes this site different: the same thought at five lengths. The slider is a standard range input. The word counts, which are the most interesting data on the page, are set in small grey type. The whole site is in lower case, which fights the subjects — Monet, NASA, Clostridium botulinum.

What each direction does. Each one gives the depth control a shape that carries a meaning: thickness, layers or time. Each one shows the index, the post page and the word motion. The text is real, from Why honey never spoils. Move the control in each demo.

Strata

A drill core through one idea. The five depths are beds of rock: the deeper you go, the thicker and the darker the bed. You see how much more text the next depth holds before you ask for it.

Colour
Limestone paper, a teal survey mark, ochre data, and a graded sediment ramp for the five beds.
Type
Big Shoulders Display for the titles, condensed like a map label.
Newsreader for the text.
Martian Mono for all data.
The control
A vertical core column. Each band is a depth, and its height follows its word count.
The risk
Data type is loud here. The page reads as a survey, not as a diary.

Rings

The name of the repository, taken at its word: a baumkuchen is a cake baked layer over layer. The depths are rings around one core. A deeper read adds a ring, and the cut face fills out from the middle.

Colour
A dark umber oven, batter and crust for the rings, and one warm honey accent.
Type
Bricolage Grotesque for the titles, soft and a little irregular.
Spectral for the text, which holds up on a dark ground.
DM Mono for the data.
The control
A ring dial. Click a ring, or use the list under it. The lit rings are what you read.
The risk
A dark page for long prose. It suits the evening, not the desk.

    The choice is a choice of time, so the page sells time. A tempo bar holds five stops, and each stop is as wide as the minutes it costs. Loud, flat and graphic, against two quiet directions.

    Colour
    Cold chalk, near-black ink, an ultramarine field and one acid mark for the stop you are on.
    Type
    Archivo Expanded for the titles, set very large.
    Instrument Sans for the text.
    DM Mono for the times. No serif at all.
    The control
    A tempo bar. The width of each stop is its reading time, so the cost is the shape.
    The risk
    The page shouts. A long read must fight the bar above it.
