The test blog
=============

A folder of posts made for the test, not for publishing. The writing is
synthetic: it was written to exercise the site, and it says nothing about any
real person, place or study.

What is here
------------

Ten articles about basic science, each written at all five depths the site
knows:

    why-the-sky-is-blue            how-magnets-work
    why-sunsets-are-red            why-thunder-follows-lightning
    how-a-rainbow-forms            how-plants-make-food
    why-ice-floats-on-water        why-the-moon-changes-shape
                                    why-hot-air-balloons-rise
                                    why-we-have-seasons

Every article carries a Sentence depth (35 words at most), a Paragraph depth
(under 120 words), a Short depth, a Medium depth and a Long depth, so the
slider has all five stops on every post. Each article also carries between two
and four pictures of its own, and links to the other articles.

The pictures live in images/ and are served at /media/, like any other picture
the site holds. They were drawn for this test: simple diagrams, made as
vector drawings and saved as PNG files.

Running it
----------

The real posts live outside this repository, behind the posts link. This folder
is a second, separate set, so it is named on the command line:

    make release
    ./build/baumkuchen -posts test-blog -addr 127.0.0.1:8080

The live site then answers on http://127.0.0.1:8080 with these ten posts. The
static build works the same way:

    ./build/baumkuchen -posts test-blog -export out

The audit reads this folder as well, and it is the one that decides whether
the writing holds to the rules of the site:

    POSTS=test-blog IMAGES=test-blog/images scripts/audit.sh

Nothing here changes the real posts, the link that points at them, or the
built site in dist/.
